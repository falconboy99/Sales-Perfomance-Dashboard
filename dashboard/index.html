Business insights

Drawn from the full four years of data.

Revenue grew 51% from 2015 to 2018$484K to $733K, with 29% growth in 2017 and 20% in 2018. Profit nearly doubled, $49.6K to $93.4K.
Discounts above 20% destroy profitLines discounted 21 to 80% lost $135K on $362K of revenue. Undiscounted lines earn a 29.5% margin.
Furniture sells but barely earnsFurniture is 32% of revenue at a 2.5% margin. Tables (-$17.7K) and Bookcases (-$3.5K) lose money outright.
Central region lags on marginCentral earns 7.9% versus West at 14.9%. Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois lose a combined $71K.
Technology and Office Supplies carry the businessTogether they produce 94% of profit. Copiers return a 37% margin; Phones and Accessories add $86K.
Sales peak from September to DecemberSeptember to December bring 52% of annual revenue. Stock and staff for the autumn peak.
